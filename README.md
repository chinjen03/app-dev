# My Favorite Series: *The Walking Dead*

> "We are the ones who live."

## Overview
**The Walking Dead** is a post-apocalyptic horror drama based on the comic book series by Robert Kirkman. It follows former sheriff's deputy **Rick Grimes** and a group of survivors as they navigate a world overrun by zombies—known as *"walkers"*—while facing even greater threats from rival human groups.

### Why I Love It
* **Character Development:** Watching characters evolve from ordinary people into hardened survivors.
* **High Stakes:** Every decision carries real consequences—~~nobody is completely safe~~.
* **Moral Dilemmas:** Explores what humanity looks like when society collapses.

### Top 3 Iconic Arcs
1. **The Prison & The Governor** (Seasons 3–4)
2. **Arriving at Alexandria** (Season 5)
3. **All Out War against Negan & The Saviors** (Seasons 7–8)
